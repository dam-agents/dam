## Getting in touch

Reach us in the open rather than by direct message, so the answer helps the next person too:

- **Bugs, feature requests, and proposed changes:** open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/dam-agents/dam/issues).
- **Questions and ideas:** start a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/dam-agents/dam/discussions).

To contribute code or docs, see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).
