Provider limits for all agents (v9)

Round 9. Limit bars are 12 px on every page, except the 4 px provider summary in Settings › Usage. Bars are black when fine, orange when close, red when reached, on a light tint of the same colour. Every bar shows its percentage on the right. The amounts show on hover; on Settings pages "$xx of $yy today" stays above the bar. Section 3 has a "Values" switch to also show the amounts small under each bar. Purple boxes are design notes. Black and dark mode switches are at the bottom right.

Choices

  • "$xx of $yy today" stays above the bar. The percentage sits right of the bar, in the state's colour; "left" is gone.
  • The track is a light tint of the state's colour, so one bar is one colour family.
  • The provider summary bars stay 4 px and show only a percentage; the amounts are on hover.
  • Shared component: DailyLimitMeter renders every limit meter, so the agent's Usage card changes too. Home's memory and CPU bars take the same black fill.

3 · Agent › Providers

The summary is this agent's own spend and limit. Each limit has a bar with its percentage; the amounts are on hover, or small under each bar with the "Values" switch.

Choices

  • Summary: this agent's spend and its own limit, "$4.20 of $5.00 today". "Close to the limit" or "Stopped until 02:00" shows only for this agent's own limit. With no agent limit: "$1.10 today · No limit for this agent".
  • Three bars: This agent, All agents (on this provider), Total (all your agents, every paid provider; dollar providers only). Each shows its percentage on the right, in the state's colour.
  • Amounts on hover: every bar part has a tooltip: "This agent: $1.10", "Other agents: $18.40", "Left: $0.50". With "Also small, below each bar", "$10.50 of $20.00" shows under each bar in small muted type.
  • On All agents and Total, this agent's part is solid and the other agents' part a lighter shade of the same colour, after a 2 px gap.
  • "All agents" and "Total" link to where they are set: Settings › Providers and Settings › Usage.
  • The reset time is in the help tip on "Daily limit".
