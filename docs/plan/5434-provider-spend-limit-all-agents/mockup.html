Provider limits for all agents (v5)

Third round. All limit bars are thin (6 px, 4 px in the summary), one colour per state: black when fine (switch at the bottom right to compare with brand blue), orange when close, red when reached. The track is a light tint of the same colour, so there's no grey. Figures sit in the text line above the bar. The agent's Providers page uses variant A. Purple boxes are design notes. Dark mode toggles at the bottom right.

Choices

  • The amount and "left" share one line above the bar, so the bar can be thin. "Left" sits right-aligned, at the end the bar is heading to.
  • The track is the light tint of the state's colour (blue, orange, red), so one bar is one colour family.
  • The summary bars are 4 px with no state line; their colour and the red figure say enough.
  • Shared component: DailyLimitMeter renders every limit meter, so this changes the agent's Usage card too. The Home resource bars use the same grey on purpose ("read as one design"). They stay grey unless you want them blue as well.

What was cut, and where it went

  • One summary line: "$0.50 left today · Close to the limit". The state sits next to the figure it qualifies, in the state's colour.
  • "Stops first" is not a sentence any more. The binding limit's label is in full weight, the other one muted. With only one limit there's nothing to compare, so no marker.
  • Meter rows: one line each, "This agent" or "All agents" on the left and "$1.10 / $5.00" on the right. "Left" shows only in the summary, where it matters.
  • Bar parts are interactive. On hover or keyboard focus, each part of the shared bar shows a tooltip: "This agent: $1.10", "Other agents: $18.40", and on the track "Left: $0.50". The split line is gone.
  • The link is the "All agents" label itself, with a tooltip saying where it's set. Without a shared limit, "Set for all agents" is a quiet link at the end of the summary row.
  • The reset time moves into the help tip on "Daily limit". When a limit is reached, the summary says the time instead: "Stopped until 02:00".
  • The paid-spend notice for all providers stays, as one short line: "Your spend limit (all agents): $0.30 left".
